Waitlist privacy notice

A concise explanation of the information Orthic uses for its pre-launch waitlist.

What is collected

Orthic collects only the email address you submit to the waitlist, together with the date and time it was added and a source value showing that it came from the website. The waitlist does not collect your name, school, age, location, phone number, IP address or device information as stored product data.

Why it is used

Your email address is used to send launch and closely related product updates about Orthic. It is not used for behavioural advertising and is not sold.

Storage and retention

Cloudflare hosts the public waitlist and stores submissions in its D1 database service for Orthic. Waitlist records are retained while launch updates remain relevant, then removed when they are no longer needed. A record will be removed earlier after a valid deletion or unsubscribe request.

Your choices

Every waitlist email must provide a way to unsubscribe. To request deletion, contact {{PRIVACY_CONTACT}}.

Who controls the data

Orthic controls the waitlist data. Cloudflare processes it only to provide hosting, security and database infrastructure. This notice will be updated if the storage or email-delivery arrangements materially change.
